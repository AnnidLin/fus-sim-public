# Disclaimer

`fus-sim` is research software for reproducible transcranial focused ultrasound simulation workflows.

It is not medical advice, not a clinical planning system, not a regulatory-cleared device, and not a validated authority for patient safety decisions. Do not use this repository to plan, approve, or deliver treatment.

Simulation outputs depend on assumptions about geometry, acoustic material properties, attenuation semantics, numerical resolution, boundary conditions, source placement, and thermal modeling. The repository intentionally records quality gates and evidence boundaries so exploratory outputs are not mistaken for validated defaults.

Users are responsible for validating methods, parameters, data rights, patient privacy constraints, and safety claims in their own institutional and regulatory context.
