# Journal-selection criteria for the bounded computational-methods manuscript

This document does not choose or rank a journal. Journal policies, fees and
limits must be checked when the author supplies candidate journals.

## Current package to match

- approximately 4,269 manuscript words including references;
- 210-word abstract;
- 3 main figures, 2 supplementary figures and 2 supplementary tables;
- single-case Case079 engineering result plus a nonpassing H115 external
  comparison and two-grid sensitivity observation;
- restricted Case079 input that must be obtained directly from PhysioNet;
- compact reproducibility archive that excludes raw/reconstructable anatomy and
  full pressure fields.

## Weighted screening rubric

| Criterion | Weight | Required evidence from author/journal |
|---|---:|---|
| Scope fit for computational acoustics, modelling or reproducible methods | 25 | Recent article types and aims explicitly accept computational-method studies |
| Acceptance of transparent negative/nonpassing validation results | 20 | Editorial scope does not require every comparator endpoint to pass |
| Data/code policy compatible with restricted health data | 15 | Allows repository code/metadata while source CT remains provider-controlled |
| Reporting and supplementary-material capacity | 10 | Can accommodate full failed-endpoint table, methods table and traceability supplements |
| Manuscript length/abstract/figure fit | 10 | Limits can accept the current inventory or a bounded edit |
| Reproducibility and software-paper expectations | 10 | Policy aligns with versioned code, environment, hashes and DOI archive |
| Cost, open-access route and institutional agreement | 5 | Author/institution confirms budget or covered route |
| Review timeline and audience | 5 | Author confirms timing and desired readership |

Score only from current official journal instructions; record the access date.

## Immediate disqualifiers

- requires public redistribution of the restricted raw CT;
- requires claiming successful experimental validation for this article type;
- requires formal convergence already absent from the evidence;
- cannot publish complete failed endpoints or essential limitations;
- licence/archiving policy conflicts with the chosen project licence or UCL
  attribution requirements.

## Author input needed before screening

Provide 2–4 candidate journals, maximum acceptable article-processing charge,
open-access requirement, preferred audience and desired submission date. Only
then should current official author instructions be checked and scored.
